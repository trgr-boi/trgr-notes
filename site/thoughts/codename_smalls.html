Codename Smalls

16/09/2026

I am starting a new music project. This will be a simple one. There are a couple of tracks already, but none of them are really finished.
This project stared because I wanted to start using the hardware I have a bit more. I wanted to stop fine-tuning everything and just quickly make some things that I like and just sound fun. Simple groovy beats, not necessarily in any specific genre, or having a complex production or something. Just having some fun with hardware and software. Making small, meaningless "garbage" just to have fun.

Here is one preview of the project. You can also find it in my Music Snippets Tool. This one is made in Renoise, a modern tracker-style DAW, syncing with my Behringer TD-3-AM that has some mods.
I will release some notes soon on my mods on the TD-3, but they are just some common ones to change the sound a little bit. There are many guides by just searching "TD-3 mods". I used the guide from matrixsynth (you can find the PDF next to the image), and the guide from maffez. Highly recommend them!

I will drop some more updates in the future, probably mixed with some experimenting I am doing.

See you soon,
TRGR.
